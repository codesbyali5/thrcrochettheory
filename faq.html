Frequently asked

Before you start stitching.

Are these physical products?

No. The store is designed for digital crochet pattern files.

How do I access a pattern?

Sign in and purchase your pattern through secure checkout. Once payment is confirmed, the purchased PDF appears in your account library.

Can I download again?

Yes. Sign in to your account library to download your purchased patterns again. Contact support if you need help accessing a file.

Which skill level should I choose?

Start with Beginner if you are comfortable with basic stitches but prefer guided construction. Easy is suited to confident beginners, while Intermediate patterns assume more independent pattern reading.

Can I print the PDF?

Yes, downloadable PDF patterns are commonly designed to be printable as well as readable on a phone, tablet or computer.
