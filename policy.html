Digital products

Products presented on THE CROCHET THEORY are intended to be downloadable crochet-pattern files, not physical finished items. Customers should review the pattern description, skill level, materials and format before purchasing.

Refunds

Because downloadable digital files can be accessed immediately, refund eligibility should follow your local consumer laws and clearly published business policy. Avoid promising “no refunds” where local law provides mandatory rights.

Usage

Unless you choose otherwise, pattern files should be licensed for personal use. Your final production policy should clearly explain whether customers may sell finished handmade items created from your patterns.

Privacy

Accounts, purchases, reviews and contact messages are stored securely by the store. Your browser retains basket and wishlist preferences. Session cookies are used to keep you signed in. Contact the store for privacy and account requests.

Payments

International card payments use a Payoneer-hosted payment page when the store's Payoneer account is eligible and configured. THE CROCHET THEORY does not receive or store card numbers, expiry dates or CVV. Downloads become available only after the payment provider confirms the matching order.
